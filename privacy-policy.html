Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Introduction

Liberty Geotechnics Ltd respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This policy explains what information may be handled when you contact us through the website.

2. Information you provide

If you use the enquiry form, the details you enter are used to prepare an email to our published business email address. The form currently does not submit information to or store it on this website.

3. Cookies

This website uses local browser storage to remember your cookie consent choice. The current site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser may also process essential technical data when loading the website.

4. How information is used

Information you choose to send to us may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss project requirements and provide our services.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information sent by email is handled through the email services and systems used by the sender and recipient. Retention depends on the purpose and applicable legal or business requirements.

6. Your choices

You may choose not to submit information through the website. You can manage or clear browser storage and cookies through your browser settings.

7. Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact libertygeotechnicsltd@gmail.com.

This is a starter policy for the website and should be reviewed against the company’s actual data practices and applicable Kenyan data protection requirements before launch.
