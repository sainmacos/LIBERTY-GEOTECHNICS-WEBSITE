About Liberty Geotechnics Ltd

Grounded in Excellence

Practical geotechnical expertise for better-built projects

Liberty Geotechnics Ltd is a Kenya-based geotechnical services company providing field exploration, laboratory soil testing and consultancy. We help clients understand ground conditions and use that information to support responsible planning, design and construction.

Our approach is built around technical care, clear reporting, responsive communication and services tailored to project requirements.

Our commitment

  • Quality-focused investigation and testing
  • Professional and transparent communication
  • Practical, project-relevant recommendations
  • Safety-conscious field operations
