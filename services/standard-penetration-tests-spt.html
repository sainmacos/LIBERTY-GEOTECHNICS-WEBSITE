Standard Penetration Tests (SPT)

SPT testing measures soil resistance at depth and provides samples and field data used in geotechnical design.

Our team works to deliver practical, project-focused information that helps clients understand ground conditions and make informed engineering decisions. Scope, methodology and deliverables are agreed to suit the site and project requirements.

How this service supports your project

  • Better understanding of subsurface conditions
  • Evidence to support engineering assessment and design
  • Clear communication of observations and test results
  • Project-specific scope and reporting
